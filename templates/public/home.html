About the Nexlink app

Nexlink (the NexlinkApp) is an online messenger built for people who want a calm, fast chat experience without noise. The Nexlink web app runs in any modern browser, keeps your conversations in sync across devices, and puts privacy controls in your hands: choose who sees your last seen, your online status and your read receipts.

Create a free Nexlink account with your phone number, find people by name or number, and start chatting in seconds.
